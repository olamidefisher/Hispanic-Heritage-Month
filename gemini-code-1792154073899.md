## World Bank Indicator Mappings & Precise Definitions

| Short Name | Indicator Code | Official World Bank Definition | Native Units | Notes & Misconception Corrections |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Life Expectancy** | `SP.DYN.LE00.IN` | Life expectancy at birth, total (years) | Years | Average lifespan expected at birth under current mortality rates. |
| **Unemployment Rate** | `SL.UEM.TOTL.NE.ZS` | Unemployment, total (% of total labor force) (national estimate) | % of labor force | Based on national household surveys and official estimates. |
| **GDP per Capita** | `NY.GDP.PCAP.CD` | Gross Domestic Product per capita (current US$) | Current USD ($) | Total GDP divided by midyear population. **Not** average household income or personal wealth. |
| **Crude Birth Rate** | `SP.DYN.CBRT.IN` | Birth rate, crude (per 1,000 people) | Per 1,000 people | Annual live births per 1,000 midyear population. **Not** raw total birth count. |
| **Net Migration** | `SM.POP.NETM` | Net migration (total over 5-year periods / annual estimates) | Persons | Total arrivals minus total departures. **Not** gross emigration count. |
| **Homicide Rate** | `VC.IHR.PSRC.P5` | Intentional homicides (per 100,000 people) | Per 100,000 people | Unlawful death inflicted upon a person with the intent to cause death or serious injury. |
| **Inflation Rate** | `FP.CPI.TOTL.ZG` | Inflation, consumer prices (annual %) | Annual % | Measured by the annual percentage change in the cost to the average consumer. |
| **Government Debt** | `GC.DOD.TOTL.GD.ZS` | Central government debt, total (% of GDP) | % of GDP | Total stock of direct government contractual financial obligations. |
| **Total Reserves** | `FI.RES.TOTL.CD` | Total reserves (includes gold, current US$) | Current USD ($) | Foreign assets held by central bank including Special Drawing Rights and gold. |
| **External Balance** | `NE.RSB.GNFS.ZS` | External balance on goods and services (% of GDP) | % of GDP | Exports of goods and services minus imports of goods and services. |

## Data Collection Protocol
- **Historical Horizon:** Current year minus 70 years through current year minus 1 year (70 discrete query years).
- **API Endpoint:** `https://api.worldbank.org/v2/country/{country}/indicator/{indicator}?date={start}:{end}&format=json&per_page=1000`
- **API Retrieval Policy:** No artificial substitution or random value generation is performed. Nulls and missing observations are filtered transparently, and actual non-null observation counts are reported directly to the user.
